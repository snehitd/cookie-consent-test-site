Test Checklist

  • E1   First visit — Klaro banner appears automatically (no prior consent in localStorage)
  • E2   Decline All — no analytics/marketing cookies set; dashboard shows all services rejected
  • E3   Google after decline — Platform Signals shows analytics_storage: denied, ad_storage: denied
  • E4   Meta after decline — fbq not loaded (blocked); or fbq queue: consent revoke
  • E5   Bing after decline — uetq not loaded (blocked); or convConsentMode: false
  • E6   Clarity after decline — window.clarity is undefined (script fully blocked)
  • E7   Accept All — _ga, _gcl_au, _fbp, _uetsid/_uetvid, _clck/_clsk all appear in Cookies panel
  • E8   Google after accept — analytics_storage: granted, ad_storage: granted in dataLayer
  • E9   Meta after accept — fbq loaded, fbq('consent','grant') called, PageView fired
  • E10   Bing after accept — convConsentMode: true in uetq
  • E11   Clarity after accept — window.clarity is a function
  • E12   Partial — accept Analytics only → _ga set; _fbp and _uetsid absent
  • E13   Revisit without reset — no banner (consent persisted in localStorage)
  • E14   Reset All → reload → banner reappears (E1 repeated)
  • E15   Manual API add: use POST /addCookies to add a custom cookie → delete consent manager from DB → rescan → manual cookie still present (not overwritten)
