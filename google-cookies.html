Google Consent Mode v2 — How It Gates These Cookies

Consent Signal Cookies Gated Behaviour When Denied
analytics_storage _ga, _ga_<ID> GA4 still sends pings but uses cookieless modelling; no client ID stored
ad_storage _gcl_au, _gcl_aw Conversion linker and click ID cookies not written; conversions modelled
ad_user_data (no cookie; signal only) User data not sent to Google Ads for audience building
ad_personalization (no cookie; signal only) Remarketing and personalised ads disabled
functionality_storage Site functional cookies (not Google-set) Not applicable to Google scripts

Note: gtm-session-start is a first-party GTM cookie — it is not gated by consent signals and fires whenever the GTM Conversion Linker tag runs.
